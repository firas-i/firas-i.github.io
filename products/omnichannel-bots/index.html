Channels we deliver today

Why one assistant, not six

Consistency is the product.

One source of answers

Update a price or an opening time once, and every channel gives the new answer.

One queue for staff

Requests from every channel arrive in a single list with the channel, contact and what was asked.

One set of limits

The same written rules about what the assistant will not do apply everywhere it speaks.

Arabic and English

Built Arabic-first, including dialect, and comfortable when a customer switches language mid-conversation.

Delivery

How we roll it out.

  1. Scope

    Which questions it answers, which requests it takes, and what it must refuse.

  2. First channel

    We launch on the channel that carries the most traffic and tune it on real conversations.

  3. Add channels

    The remaining channels are connected to the same assistant, one at a time.

  4. Operate

    Monitoring, updates to your information, and regular review of real transcripts.

Pricing depends on the number of channels and the volume of conversations. Connecting some channels requires business accounts with the platform owner, such as a WhatsApp Business account. We guide you through that setup.
