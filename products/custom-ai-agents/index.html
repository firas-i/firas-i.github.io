Custom AI Agents

When a ready-made bot does not fit, we build an agent around one specific job in your organisation: its inputs, its rules, its systems and the person who takes over when it stops.

Describe your workflow

Good candidates

Work an agent can take on.

These are examples of the kind of task that suits an agent, not a list of past projects.

Intake and triage
Reading incoming requests, forms or emails, extracting the details and routing each one to the right person.
Front-line answers
Answering customer or employee questions from your own documents and policies.
Scheduling and follow-up
Collecting availability, sending reminders and chasing the replies nobody has time to chase.
Document preparation
Drafting routine documents from structured data for a person to review and approve.
Internal assistants
Helping staff find procedures, past decisions and records across the systems you already have.

What every agent ships with

Four things you can hold us to.

A written scope

What the agent does, what it refuses, and when it stops and hands over. Agreed before build.

A test set

Scenarios, including attempts to push it past its limits, that are re-run after every change.

A hand-over

A queue or notification that gives your staff everything they need to finish the job.

A record

Conversations and actions are logged so you can review what the agent did and why.

Process

From one workflow to a working agent.

  1. Choose the job

    One task with a clear owner and a clear definition of done.

  2. Write the rules

    Scope, refusals and hand-over, reviewed with the people who do the work today.

  3. Build and connect

    The agent, plus the integrations to your channels and systems.

  4. Test and launch

    Evaluated against the test set, then released to real use under watch.

Custom agents are scoped and priced per project. This product is the delivery side of our AI & Intelligent Automation service. If you are still deciding what to automate, start there.
