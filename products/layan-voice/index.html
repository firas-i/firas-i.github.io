On a call

What Layan does when the phone rings.

Answers every call
After hours, on Fridays and during the rush, when the front desk cannot pick up.
Answers from your information
Hours, location, services and the prices you set. If it does not have an answer it gives the reception number.
Takes appointment requests
What the caller needs and when suits them, with the phone number read back to confirm it.
Does not transfer calls
It gives the reception number, or takes a message and your team calls back.
Stops for emergencies
If the caller describes a threat to life, Layan stops everything and tells them to call emergency services on 911.
