Layan Chat

Layan answers your clinic’s WhatsApp within seconds, at any hour, in Jordanian Arabic. It handles the common questions and takes appointment requests. Your receptionist confirms them in the morning.

How a conversation goes

Three things Layan does in every chat.

  1. Introduces itself and answers

    It says it is an AI assistant in its first message, then answers from the hours, services and prices the clinic provided.

  2. Takes a request, not a booking

    It records what the patient wants and when suits them. Layan does not see the schedule, so confirming the time stays with reception.

  3. Asks little, and never pushes

    A suitable time and how to reach the patient. It does not ask about medical conditions or collect details it does not need.

Layan does

  • Answer repeated questions from your clinic’s information: hours, address, services, and the prices you choose to share
  • Take appointment requests and messages for the doctor
  • Direct life-threatening situations to emergency services immediately
  • Speak Jordanian Arabic, and continue in English if the patient switches

Layan does not

  • Diagnose or give medical advice
  • Name any medicine or painkiller
  • Confirm an appointment by itself, or promise a specific call-back time
  • Guess a price or a fact it was not given

The morning after

Reception opens to a ready list.

Every conversation becomes an open request in one list. The receptionist calls, confirms the time and closes it. Layan does not replace her. It prepares her morning.
