How we work

Three commitments we make on every engagement.

We build what we recommend
Advice is cheap when someone else has to make it work. Our recommendations come with an offer to deliver them, and we write them accordingly.
We start small and finish
The first project is sized to be completed and judged. It earns the right to a second one.
We write the limits down
Every system we ship, especially an AI one, comes with a plain statement of what it does, what it will not do, and when a person takes over.
