AI & Intelligent Automation

We design, build and operate AI agents and automations that take work off your team. Every one ships with written limits: what it may do, what it must never do, and when it hands over to a person.

Discuss this service

The problem

Where this usually starts.

Demos are easy. The hard part is an assistant that behaves the same way on its thousandth conversation as on its first, admits what it does not know, and leaves a record your staff can act on.

What we do

The work, in plain terms.

Use-case selection
We look for tasks that are frequent, bounded and currently handled late or not at all. Those are where automation earns its keep first.
Conversational agents
Chat and voice assistants that answer from your own information and collect structured requests. Our Layan products are built this way.
Workflow automation
Document intake, classification, routing, reminders and follow-ups connected to the systems you already run.
Guardrails and escalation
Refusal rules, safety routing and human hand-off are designed before the first prompt is written.
Evaluation and monitoring
Test conversations, regression checks on every change, and a review loop so behaviour improves instead of drifting.

How an engagement runs

4 stages, each with something you can review.

  1. Pick one task

    A single job with a clear owner and a clear definition of done.

  2. Write the rules

    What the agent does, what it refuses, and where the person takes over.

  3. Build and test

    A working agent, tested against realistic and adversarial conversations.

  4. Run and tune

    We monitor real usage with you and adjust in the first weeks.

What you receive

  • A working agent or automation in your channels
  • Written scope and refusal rules
  • Test set you can re-run after every change
  • Hand-over queue for your staff
