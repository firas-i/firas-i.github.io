Business Analysis & Process Design

We translate between the people who run the operation and the people who build the software, so the thing delivered is the thing that was needed.

Discuss this service

The problem

Where this usually starts.

The project brief says "a portal". Three departments mean three different things by it. The gap is discovered at user acceptance, when it is most expensive to close.

What we do

The work, in plain terms.

Requirements discovery
Workshops and interviews that surface what people actually need, including the needs they have stopped noticing.
Process mapping and redesign
Current and future-state flows, with the handovers and exceptions that usually get left out.
Functional specifications
User stories, acceptance criteria and data definitions written to be built and tested against.
Backlog and scope management
A prioritised backlog and a clear line between this release and the next.
Acceptance support
Test scenarios for the business, and help running user acceptance properly.

How an engagement runs

4 stages, each with something you can review.

  1. Discover

    Who is affected, what they do today, what they need.

  2. Model

    Processes and data drawn out and validated with the people in them.

  3. Specify

    Requirements with acceptance criteria and priorities.

  4. Validate

    We stay through build and acceptance to keep intent and delivery aligned.

What you receive

  • Process maps, current and future state
  • Requirements with acceptance criteria
  • Prioritised backlog
  • User acceptance scenarios
