Service 03

Technology Consulting & Architecture

When you are about to commit budget to a platform, a vendor or a rebuild, we give you a second opinion grounded in how the system will actually be built and run.

Discuss this service

The problem

Where this usually starts.

Technology decisions are expensive to reverse. Vendors have a product to sell and internal teams have habits to protect. You need someone whose only interest is that the thing works.

What we do

The work, in plain terms.

Architecture review
We read the design, the code and the infrastructure, then tell you where it will bend and where it will break.
Solution design
Target architecture for a new system: components, data flows, hosting, security boundaries and a build sequence.
Vendor and platform selection
Requirements, a shortlist, structured demos and a recommendation with the trade-offs spelled out.
Technical due diligence
An independent read on a product or codebase before you buy, partner or inherit it.
Fractional technical leadership
A senior technical voice in your planning and vendor meetings, for teams not ready to hire one full time.

How an engagement runs

4 stages, each with something you can review.

  1. Frame the decision

    What exactly is being decided, by when, and what would make it a mistake.

  2. Gather evidence

    Documents, code, demos and conversations with the people who will live with the outcome.

  3. Recommend

    A written recommendation with alternatives and the reasons we set them aside.

  4. Support

    We stay available through procurement and the first build milestones.

What you receive

  • Written recommendation with trade-offs
  • Architecture diagrams and decision records
  • Risk register with mitigations
  • Build sequence and rough effort
