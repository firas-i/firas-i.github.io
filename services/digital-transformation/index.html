Service 01

Digital Transformation Strategy

Most transformation plans fail in the gap between the slide and the system. We write strategy that an engineering team can start building on Monday, and we stay to build it.

Discuss this service

The problem

Where this usually starts.

You know the organisation needs to modernise. What you do not have is an honest picture of where the work actually happens today, which changes will pay back, and in what order to make them.

What we do

The work, in plain terms.

Current-state assessment
We sit with the people doing the work and map how requests, records and decisions really move, including the spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups nobody admits to.
Opportunity sizing
Each candidate change gets a plain estimate: effort, risk, who it affects, and what you would measure to prove it helped.
Roadmap and sequencing
A short, ordered list of initiatives. The first one is always small enough to finish and visible enough to earn trust for the second.
Build-or-buy decisions
When an off-the-shelf tool is the right answer we say so, even when it means less work for us.
Change and adoption planning
Training, ownership and the awkward first month are planned up front, not discovered after go-live.

How an engagement runs

4 stages, each with something you can review.

  1. Listen

    Interviews and observation with the teams who own the process. No questionnaires sent from a distance.

  2. Map

    A current-state map and a list of friction points, reviewed with you until it matches reality.

  3. Decide

    A ranked roadmap with one recommended first project and the reasoning behind the order.

  4. Deliver

    We can build the first initiative ourselves, or hand over a brief any competent team can execute.

What you receive

  • Current-state process map
  • Ranked opportunity list with effort and risk
  • 6 to 12 month roadmap
  • A scoped brief for the first project
