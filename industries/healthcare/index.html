Clinics close. Patients do not stop asking. We help private practices answer every enquiry, lighten the load on reception, and adopt AI without taking clinical risks.

What we see

The front desk is where clinics lose patients.

Enquiries arrive after hours
People message and call when they remember: after work, late at night, on the weekend. An unanswered patient asks the next clinic.
Reception is overloaded at peak
The same questions about prices, hours and location are answered dozens of times, while the phone keeps ringing.
Tools do not connect
Messages, calls and the appointment book live in separate places, and the receptionist is the integration.
AI raises fair concerns
Owners worry, correctly, about an assistant giving medical advice or making promises the clinic cannot keep.
