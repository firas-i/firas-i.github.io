About

A small firm that does the work itself.

All In is a founder-led technology consulting firm based in Amman, Jordan. We advise on digital transformation, and we design, build and test the systems that advice leads to.

Why we exist

Advice and delivery belong together.

Too many organisations pay for a strategy from one firm and a build from another, and then spend a year discovering that the two do not meet. We think the people who recommend a system should be prepared to build it and be judged on whether it works.

Our background is in building AI systems, integrating business software, testing it properly and analysing the processes it serves. Those four disciplines are the firm.

We also run our own products. Layan, our AI receptionist for clinics, is how we test our methods on ourselves before we recommend them to anyone else.

Principles

How we behave.

Plain language
If we cannot explain it without jargon, we do not understand it well enough to sell it.
Honest scope
We say what we have not done before. We recommend an off-the-shelf tool when it is the better answer.
No invented proof
No fabricated testimonials, client logos or statistics. What we show is real, or it is labelled as an example.
Safety before features
For AI systems, the limits are designed first and tested on every change.
You own the result
Code, documentation and data belong to you at the end of the engagement.

Being early

What a young firm means for you.

We are at the beginning, and we think that works in your favour. You speak directly with the person building your solution, not with an account manager. Decisions are quick. Your project is not one of two hundred.

It also means we choose engagements carefully and start with a scope we are certain we can deliver.
